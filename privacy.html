Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

AlphaCore Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with our AI-powered services, including ChatCore (SMS/WhatsApp messaging assistant) and VoiceCore (AI voice assistant for phone calls).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect your phone number, name, and the content of messages or calls when you interact with our services. This includes text messages sent via SMS or WhatsApp (ChatCore) and voice call recordings or transcripts (VoiceCore). This information is provided when you initiate contact with us or one of our business clients.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information primarily to respond to your inquiries, confirm appointments, provide customer service, and send relevant business notifications you have requested. We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data may be processed by third-party service providers strictly to deliver our services.

4. Data Retention

We retain message data only as long as necessary to provide the service.

5. SMS / Text Messaging Program

How you opt in: By submitting the contact form at alphacoresolutions.co/contact with your phone number, you expressly consent to receive automated SMS text messages from AlphaCore Solutions. Opt-in is voluntary — providing your phone number is optional.

What you'll receive: Appointment confirmations, business hour updates, service notifications, and follow-up messages related to your inquiry. We do not send unsolicited marketing SMS.

Message frequency: Varies based on your interactions with our services.

Message and data rates: Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Opt out: Reply STOP to any message at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive one final confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

Help: Reply HELP to any message or contact us at 612-324-4413 or info@alphacoresolutions.co.

We do not share your phone number or SMS consent with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights

You may contact us with questions about how your data is handled at info@alphacoresolutions.co or call 612-324-4413.
